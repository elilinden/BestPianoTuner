What You Need

The app handles pitch detection — you handle the tools.

  • 🔧
    Tuning lever (tuning hammer) The T-shaped wrench used to turn tuning pins. Available online or at music shops.
  • 🧱
    Rubber or felt mutes Used to silence adjacent strings so you can tune one string at a time.
  • 🤫
    A quiet room Background noise from fans, AC, or TVs can interfere with pitch detection.
  • 📱
    iPhone or iPad (iOS 26+) Place it within 1–2 feet of the piano for best results.
  • ⏳
    Patience and realistic expectations A full tuning takes 1–2 hours. Start with a touch-up and work up from there.

Safety & Limitations

What the app can and can't do.

Important: Best Piano Tuner provides accurate pitch detection and guided workflows, but improper technique can still damage piano strings or tuning pins. The app is a tool — not a substitute for learning proper tuning mechanics.
  • ✅ The app includes safety warnings and beginner-friendly guardrails throughout the tuning process.
  • ✅ Single Note and Quick Touch-Up modes let you make small adjustments with low risk.
  • ⚠️ If your piano hasn't been tuned in years or is more than a semitone flat, consider hiring a professional for the first pass.
  • ⚠️ Over-tightening strings can cause them to break. Always turn the pin slowly and in small increments.
  • ⚠️ The app detects pitch, not string tension. Use your ears and judgment alongside the app's feedback.
