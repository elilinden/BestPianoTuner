Frequently Asked Questions

The tuner isn't detecting my note accurately.
Make sure you're in a quiet room and your iPhone is within 1–2 feet of the piano. Avoid background noise like fans or air conditioning. If using an older device, try placing it closer to the strings.
Which tuning mode should I use?
If you're new to piano tuning, start with Single Note mode to get comfortable. Use Quick Touch-Up to fix a few problem notes. Use Full Tuning for a complete guided session through all 88 keys.
What does the AR Pin Finder do?
It uses your camera to help you identify which tuning pin belongs to which note inside the piano. Open the piano lid, point your camera at the tuning pins, and the app will try to detect and map them.
Do I need special equipment?
You need a tuning lever (also called a tuning hammer) and rubber or felt mutes. The app replaces the electronic tuning device but not the physical tools.
Can this app damage my piano?
The app includes built-in safety warnings and beginner-friendly workflows. However, improper tuning technique can damage strings or pins. If your piano is severely out of tune or hasn't been tuned in years, we recommend hiring a professional for the first tuning.
Does the app work offline?
Yes. Best Piano Tuner works entirely offline. No internet connection is required.
