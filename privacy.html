Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 13, 2026

Best Piano Tuner ("the App") is developed and maintained by Eli Linden.

Information We Collect

Best Piano Tuner does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All data stays on your device.

Microphone Access

The App requests microphone permission solely to detect pitch from your piano in real time. Audio is processed entirely on-device and is never recorded, stored, or sent to any server.

Camera Access

The App requests camera permission for the AR Pin Finder feature. Camera data is processed on-device using Apple's ARKit and Vision frameworks. No images or video are recorded, stored, or transmitted.

Location Access

If enabled, location data is used only to estimate local temperature and humidity for the Climate Check feature. Location data is not stored or shared.

Data Storage

All app data — including piano profiles, tuning history, and settings — is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage. No data is sent to external servers.

Third-Party Services

Best Piano Tuner does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The App collects no personal information from any user.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact
