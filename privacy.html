Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 13, 2026

Best Piano Tuner ("the App") is developed and maintained by Eli Linden. Your privacy matters — here's the short version:

🔒

No Personal Data

We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information.

📵

No Tracking

No analytics, ads, or third-party tracking services.

📱

On-Device Processing

Core tuning features work entirely on your device, with no internet required.

🌤️

One Optional Network Feature

Climate Check can fetch weather data over the internet if you use it.

Offline by Default

All core tuning features — pitch detection, guided tuning sessions, AR Pin Finder, Learn Mode, piano profiles, calibration, and stretch curves — work entirely offline with no internet connection required. The App does not require an account, login, or sign-up.

Device Permissions

The App requests a few permissions to function. Here's exactly what each one does and why:

🎤 Microphone

Used solely to detect pitch from your piano in real time. Audio is processed entirely on-device and is never recorded, stored, or sent to any server.

📷 Camera

Used for the AR Pin Finder feature to help identify tuning pins inside your piano. Camera data is processed on-device using Apple's ARKit and Vision frameworks. No images or video are recorded, stored, or transmitted.

📍 Location (Optional)

If you enable it, location data is used only by the Climate Check feature to estimate local temperature and humidity. See the "Online Features" section below for details on how location data is handled.

This permission is entirely optional. Without it, automatic weather lookup is unavailable, but you can still enter temperature and humidity values manually. All tuning features work fully without location access.

Online Features

🌤️ Climate Check — Weather Data Lookup

The Climate Check feature fetches local temperature and humidity data so you can assess how environmental conditions may affect your piano's tuning stability. Here is exactly what happens when you use this feature:

  • What is sent: Your approximate latitude and longitude coordinates are sent to the weather provider to retrieve local conditions.
  • When it is sent: Only when you actively open or refresh the Climate Check screen. No background requests are made.
  • Which provider: Weather data is fetched from Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com), a free, open-source weather API.
  • What Open-Meteo receives: Your approximate coordinates and the weather data request. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy applies to their handling of this data. We encourage you to review it at open-meteo.com/en/terms.
  • What we store: We do not operate any servers. We do not store, log, or retain any weather lookup data or location coordinates. The weather result is displayed in the app and not persisted.
  • Is it optional? Yes. Climate Check is entirely optional. You can skip it entirely and all tuning features will work normally. If you deny location permission, automatic weather lookup is unavailable, but you can still enter temperature and humidity manually.

This is the only feature in Best Piano Tuner that makes a network request. All other features work completely offline.

Local Data Storage

All app data — including piano profiles, tuning history, session results, calibration data, and settings — is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage frameworks (such as SwiftData or UserDefaults). This data is:

  • Stored only on your iPhone or iPad
  • Not synced to any external server or cloud service operated by us
  • Fully under your control — you can delete it at any time by removing the app

Third-Party Services

Best Piano Tuner does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs. The only external service the App communicates with is the Open-Meteo weather API, and only when you use the Climate Check feature.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13. Because no personal data is collected, the App complies with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) by design.

Your Rights

Since Best Piano Tuner does not collect or process any personal data on our end, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, export, or delete. All data stored by the app lives on your device and is fully under your control. You can delete all app data at any time by removing the app from your device.

App Store Privacy Labels

Our App Store privacy answers reflect this policy. If you notice a discrepancy between this page and the App Store privacy labels, please contact us so we can correct it.

Changes to This Policy
