Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 13, 2026

Best Piano Tuner ("the App") is developed and maintained by Eli Linden. Your privacy matters — here's the short version:

🔒

No Data Collection

We collect zero personal information.

📵

No Tracking

No analytics, ads, or third-party services.

📱

On-Device Only

All data stays on your iPhone or iPad.

🌐

No Servers

Nothing is sent over the internet.

Device Permissions

The App requests a few permissions to function. Here's exactly what each one does and why:

🎤 Microphone

Used solely to detect pitch from your piano in real time. Audio is processed entirely on-device and is never recorded, stored, or sent to any server.

📷 Camera

Used for the AR Pin Finder feature to help identify tuning pins inside your piano. Camera data is processed on-device using Apple's ARKit and Vision frameworks. No images or video are recorded, stored, or transmitted.

📍 Location (Optional)

If you enable it, location data is used only to estimate local temperature and humidity for the Climate Check feature. Location data is not stored or shared. This permission is entirely optional and the app works fully without it.

Data Storage

All app data — including piano profiles, tuning history, and settings — is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage frameworks. No data is synced to external servers or cloud services.

Third-Party Services

Best Piano Tuner does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking services. The App makes no network requests.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13. Because no data is collected, the App complies with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) by design.

Your Rights

Since Best Piano Tuner does not collect or process any personal data, there is no personal data to access, correct, export, or delete. You are in full control of all data stored on your device and can delete it at any time by removing the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
